## Featured Project

### Employee Attrition Prediction
*An ML pipeline that doesn't just predict who's at risk of leaving — it explains why, in terms an HR team can act on.*

**The problem** — Attrition models are usually judged purely on accuracy, which is misleading on a dataset this imbalanced (~6:1 stayed vs. left), and often produce a risk score with no explanation behind it.

**The approach** — Cleaned and encoded 35 raw HR attributes, then trained and compared three models (Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest) across five metrics (accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC) rather than optimizing for one number in isolation.

**The result** — Logistic Regression led on every relevant metric (86% accuracy, 0.81 ROC-AUC) *and* is fully interpretable — no tradeoff needed. The final deliverable is a signed feature-importance chart ranking exactly what raises or lowers attrition risk.
